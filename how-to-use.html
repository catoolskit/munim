How to use MUNIM

Step-by-step guide to set up MUNIM and start entering your accounts from day one.

1. Install 2. Create Company 3. Sales Entry 4. Purchase Entry 5. View Reports 6. Backup Data FAQ
Step 1
Download and install MUNIM

No installation wizard needed. Just download and double-click.

1
Download MUNIM.exe
Click the Download button on this website. The file is about 25 MB. Save it anywhere on your computer — Desktop is easiest.
2
Double-click MUNIM.exe to open
No installation needed. MUNIM opens directly. Windows may show a SmartScreen warning — click "More info" then "Run anyway". This is normal for new software.
💡 Windows SmartScreen warning is safe to dismiss — MUNIM has no virus or malware.
3
Data folder created automatically
On first launch, MUNIM creates a folder at C:\MUNIM\ and sets up the database. This is where all your data lives. You never need to touch this folder manually.
System Requirements
Windows 10 or Windows 11 · Minimum 4GB RAM · 100MB free disk space · No internet required
Step 2
Create your company

Set up your business profile once — MUNIM uses it on all invoices and reports.

1
Click "Create New Company"
On the first screen, click "Create New Company". If you already have a company, click "Open Company" to load it.
2
Fill in your business details
Enter: Business Name, Address, State, GSTIN (if registered), PAN Number, Financial Year (April to March), and your opening cash balance.
3
Set admin username and password
Create a login ID and password for your company. This protects your books. You can add more users (staff) later from Settings.
💡 Keep your password safe — MUNIM stores it locally and cannot recover it if lost.
4
Company is ready
MUNIM creates all default account groups (Assets, Liabilities, Income, Expenses) and default ledgers automatically. Your dashboard is ready to use.
Step 3
How to enter a sales bill

Every bill you create automatically posts to the Sales Register, Party Ledger, and Trial Balance.

A
Create a new sales bill
  • Click "Sales" in the left menu
  • Click "New Sales Entry" button
  • Select party name from dropdown (or add new)
  • Enter bill date
  • Add items — name, quantity, rate, GST %
  • MUNIM calculates total and GST automatically
  • Click "Save Bill" — it posts everywhere instantly
B
Print or share the invoice
  • After saving, click "Print Invoice"
  • Choose A4 GST Invoice or Thermal Slip
  • Your printer opens automatically
  • Or click "Save as PDF" to share on WhatsApp
  • Invoice includes your GSTIN, HSN codes, tax split
Pro Tip
Add your common items (like Gold Ring, Silver Chain) to the Item Master once. Then just select them from the dropdown when creating bills — no retyping needed.
Step 4
How to record a purchase

Record supplier purchases — stock and ledger update automatically.

A
Enter a purchase entry
  • Click "Purchase" in the left menu
  • Click "New Purchase Entry"
  • Select or add supplier name
  • Enter supplier invoice number and date
  • Add items purchased with quantity and rate
  • Click "Save" — stock and ledger update instantly
B
Record a payment to supplier
  • Click "Vouchers" → "Payment"
  • Select supplier from dropdown
  • Enter amount paid and payment mode (Cash/Bank)
  • Add narration (optional)
  • Click "Save" — supplier ledger updates
Step 5
How to view reports

All reports pull live data — no manual work needed.

A
View Trial Balance
  • Click "Reports" in the left menu
  • Click "Trial Balance"
  • Select date range (or full year)
  • MUNIM shows all accounts with Dr/Cr totals
  • Click "Export to PDF" or "Export to Excel"
B
View Balance Sheet
  • Click "Reports" → "Balance Sheet"
  • Select financial year
  • Assets and Liabilities shown as per Schedule III
  • Net Profit from P&L auto-included
  • Export to PDF to share with your CA
C
View Party Ledger
  • Click "Ledger" in left menu
  • Select party name from dropdown
  • See all transactions with opening and closing balance
  • Filter by date range
  • Print or export for sharing
Step 6
How to backup your data

Your data is at C:\MUNIM\ — backing up is as simple as copying a folder.

1
Open File Explorer → go to C:\MUNIM\
This folder contains your entire database — all companies, all entries, all data.
2
Copy the entire MUNIM folder
Right-click → Copy → Paste to a USB drive, Google Drive, or any external storage. That's your backup.
3
To restore — paste back to C:\
On a new computer, install MUNIM.exe and paste your backed-up MUNIM folder to C:\. All your data will be there when you open MUNIM.
💡 MUNIM also has a built-in backup button in Settings → Backup & Restore.
FAQ
Common questions
Does MUNIM work without internet?
Yes — 100% offline. MUNIM never connects to the internet. All data is stored on your own computer at C:\MUNIM\. No cloud, no server, no subscription.
Is MUNIM really free? Are there hidden charges?
MUNIM is completely free forever. No subscription, no trial period, no hidden charges. Download once and use it for life.
Can I use MUNIM for GST filing?
MUNIM generates GST-ready invoices and reports (GSTR-1 summary, Sales Register with GST). For actual GST filing on the portal, export your data to Excel and upload on the GST portal directly.
What if I change computers or reinstall Windows?
Just copy your C:\MUNIM\ folder to a USB drive or external storage before reinstalling. After reinstalling MUNIM on the new system, paste the folder back to C:\ and all your data will be intact.
Can my CA access my MUNIM data?
Yes — export any report (Trial Balance, Balance Sheet, Ledger) to PDF or Excel from inside MUNIM and share it with your CA on WhatsApp or email. No need for your CA to install MUNIM.
Does MUNIM work for businesses other than jewellery?
Yes. MUNIM is built for any Indian small business — retail shops, traders, service providers, manufacturers. The accounting modules (Sales, Purchase, Ledger, TB, BS) work for all types of businesses.
