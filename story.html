📖 Our Story

Why we built
MUNIM

Every small business in India deserves proper accounting software. Not a notebook. Not an Excel sheet. A real, complete tool — that works without internet and keeps your data on your own computer.

"Every small shop deserves a proper मुनीम — not just a rough notebook."
The idea that started MUNIM
The Name
What does MUNIM mean?
मुनीम
Munim — the trusted bookkeeper
In traditional Indian businesses, the मुनीम was the trusted person who kept all the accounts — recording every sale, every purchase, every transaction with care and accuracy. MUNIM is your digital मुनीम — doing the same job, automatically.
The Problem
What we saw around us
1
The small business accounting gap

Walk into any small business in India — a jewellery shop in Ludhiana, a kirana store in Jaipur, a cloth trader in Surat — and ask them how they keep accounts. Most will show you a rough notebook. Some will show you an Excel file with no structure. Very few will have proper accounting software.

It is not because they do not want proper accounts. It is because the options are too expensive, too complex, or both.

2
The real cost of bad accounting

When small businesses do not have proper accounts, real problems follow. Outstanding balances get lost. Stock figures go wrong. The CA scrambles every March to prepare financials from rough notes. GST returns get delayed. Business decisions get made on gut feel instead of actual numbers.

MUNIM exists to fix exactly this. Proper accounting, properly automated, at an affordable price.

The Solution
What MUNIM set out to be
MUNIM was built with one clear goal
  • Affordable — ₹2,499/year, all features included
  • Works 100% offline — no internet dependency
  • Auto-posting — bills flow to registers automatically
  • Complete reports — TB, P&L, Balance Sheet instantly
  • Simple enough for a shop owner with no accounting degree
  • Data stays on your computer — complete privacy
  • GST-ready — proper Indian invoices out of the box
  • Fast — runs on older computers too
Journey
How MUNIM came together
Early 2026
The idea
Frustration with existing options
Running a small jewellery business in Ludhiana meant needing proper accounts — but every option was too expensive or too complex. The idea for MUNIM was born: build it ourselves, make it affordable.
Mid 2026
Building
Core modules built from scratch
Sales, Purchase, Journal, Ledger, Trial Balance, and Balance Sheet built from scratch. The auto-posting logic — the heart of MUNIM — developed and tested extensively.
Oct 2026
Testing
Real-world testing on actual business data
MUNIM tested on actual jewellery shop data. Every edge case fixed. GST logic refined. Excel export added after real feedback from daily use.
Nov 2026
Launch
MUNIM v1 — first public release
₹2,499/year. All accounting modules, full report generation, GST invoicing, and local data storage — all in one .exe file.
2027
Coming next
