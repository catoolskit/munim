Why we built MUNIM

Every business in India deserves proper accounts — and every tax professional deserves tools that don't waste their time.

मुनीम
Munim — the trusted bookkeeper

In traditional Indian businesses, the munim kept every account with care — every sale, every purchase, every rupee. MUNIM is your digital munim: the same job, done automatically and kept private on your own computer.

Walk into a small business almost anywhere in India and ask how the accounts are kept. Most will show you a notebook. Some, an Excel sheet with no structure. Very few use proper accounting software — not because they don't want to, but because the options are too expensive, too complicated, or both.

The result is familiar: outstanding amounts get forgotten, GST returns are rushed, and every March the CA rebuilds a year of accounts from rough notes. Decisions get made on gut feeling instead of numbers.

MUNIM Books was built to fix exactly this — real double-entry accounting that posts itself, GST returns that prepare themselves, and screens a shop owner can use on day one. It runs offline, keeps every rupee of data on your own computer, and costs ₹2,499 a year.

GST invoices and returns, full reports, stock, mobile invoicing, a new interface and secure online activation.

Tax professionals spend a surprising part of their day on repetition: the same client typed into one program for income tax, another for GST and a third for TDS. Figures copied by hand from the client's books. Portal logins hunted down one by one.

From April 2026 the new Income-tax Act changes section numbers, form numbers and payment codes across TDS and income tax. That makes it the right moment to build tax software fresh — around the new law, not patched on top of the old one.

MUNIM Pro starts from one idea: enter a client once. Tax Pro, GST Pro and TDS Pro share one client list, switch from the top bar, and read data directly from clients who use MUNIM Books. It is keyboard-first, works offline, and never bypasses a captcha or OTP — you stay in control.
