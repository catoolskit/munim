Pricing FAQ

How do I pay?

WhatsApp us at +91 84276 51580. We send the payment details, and your username, password and licence key within minutes of payment.

Do I need a new key when I renew?

No. After you renew, just open MUNIM with internet ON — it picks up the new validity automatically.

How many computers can I use?

Each licence works on one computer. Need MUNIM on more computers? WhatsApp us for extra licences.

I changed my computer. Do I pay again?

No. WhatsApp us and we'll free your licence from the old computer so you can activate on the new one.

What happens if I don't renew?

MUNIM shows a renewal notice. Your data stays safe on your computer at C:\MUNIM\ and is never deleted.

Can I add a MUNIM Pro module later?

Yes. Add any module whenever you need it — WhatsApp us and we'll add it to your existing licence.
