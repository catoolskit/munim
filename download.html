Before you download

Common questions

Is my data safe?

Yes. All your data is stored on your own computer at C:\MUNIM\. Nothing is uploaded to any server — we never see your books. Only your licence is checked online.

Do I need internet to use MUNIM?

Only for the first activation. After that MUNIM works fully offline. Connect at least once every 30 days so it can quietly re-check your licence.

What happens when my licence expires?

MUNIM shows a renewal notice and your data stays safe. After you renew, just open MUNIM with internet ON — no new key needed.

How do I move MUNIM to a new computer?

WhatsApp us to free your licence from the old computer. Copy the C:\MUNIM\ folder to the new computer, download MUNIM there and activate with the same details.
