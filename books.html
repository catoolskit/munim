What MUNIM Books does

S

Sales Invoice

GST invoices for customers in seconds.

  • HSN / SAC, qty, rate, GST %
  • Several GST rates on one bill
  • Collect part payment at sale
  • Walk-in name on the bill
  • Ctrl+P saves the PDF
P

Purchase Bill

Supplier bills with input GST posted for you.

  • Input CGST / SGST / IGST split
  • Pay partly at the time of entry
  • Flows into outstanding & ageing
R

Sales & Purchase Returns

Goods back from customers or to suppliers, with GST reversed correctly.

  • Linked to the party's account
  • Shows in registers and GST
₹

Receipt & Payment

Money in and out, settled against bills automatically.

  • Oldest bill settled first
  • Extra stays as advance
  • Cash or bank
V

Voucher Entry

Journal, Payment, Receipt and Contra with full double entry.

  • Unlimited lines
  • Debit must equal credit
  • Type-to-search accounts
D

Daily Sales Summary

One entry for a whole day of small counter sales.

  • Amount per GST rate
  • Tax-inclusive or exclusive
  • Cash or bank receipt

GST that sorts itself out

Enter bills normally — MUNIM works out the tax and prepares your monthly returns.

  • CGST / SGST or IGST from the party's GSTIN state code
  • GSTIN format check on every party
  • GSTR-1 (outward supplies) every month
  • GSTR-3B summary every month
  • JSON export to upload on the GST portal
  • GST invoice PDF from any saved invoice
