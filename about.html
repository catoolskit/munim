About the Project
What MUNIM is

MUNIM is a Windows desktop accounting software built specifically for small Indian businesses — traders, shopkeepers, and small manufacturers who need proper double-entry books but don't need the complexity or cost of enterprise software.

It runs as a single .exe file, stores all data locally on your computer, and works fully offline. There is no cloud, no subscription portal, no dependency on anyone's server.

MUNIM is built and supported by Sumit Verma, a small business owner from Ludhiana, Punjab, who built it to solve his own accounting problem and decided to make it available to others.

It is part of CA Toolkit — a small suite of tools for Indian accountants and small businesses.
