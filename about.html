🏪 Our Story

Built from a real
business problem

MUNIM was born out of frustration with expensive, complex accounting software that small Indian businesses simply cannot afford — or figure out.

Why MUNIM exists
The problem with existing options

Running a small business in India — a jewellery shop, a kirana store, a small trader — means you need proper accounts. Sales register, purchase register, ledger, trial balance, balance sheet. The works.

But Tally costs ₹18,000+ per year. Busy and Evershine aren't much cheaper. And Excel means doing everything manually, with no auto-posting, no reports, no structure.

Most small business owners end up keeping rough notebooks, losing track of outstanding balances, and scrambling every March to give their CA something coherent.

MUNIM is the answer to that. A proper accounting software — with all the modules a small business actually needs — completely free, works offline, and saves everything on your own computer.

What we stand for
Our principles
🆓
Free forever — no tricks
MUNIM will always be free. No freemium, no trial, no "upgrade to unlock reports". Every feature is available from day one.
🔒
Your data is yours
We never see your data. It lives on your computer at C:\MUNIM\. No cloud upload, no analytics, no tracking. Complete privacy.
📴
Works without internet
Power cut, no WiFi, travelling — MUNIM works regardless. Your accounting should never depend on someone else's server being online.
🇮🇳
Built for India
GST invoices, Indian financial year (April–March), rupee formatting, Hindi-friendly UI. MUNIM is designed ground-up for Indian businesses.
⚡
Simple over complex
We deliberately leave out features you don't need. Every screen in MUNIM should be usable by a shop owner without any accounting training.
🤝
CA-friendly outputs
Export any report to PDF or Excel in one click. Your CA gets clean, properly formatted statements without you needing to do anything extra.
