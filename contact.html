Common Questions
Quick answers
How do I activate MUNIM after downloading?
Open MUNIM.exe — it will show an activation screen. Enter the username, password and licence key we sent you. Done in 30 seconds.
I forgot my password. What do I do?
WhatsApp us with your username. We'll reset it and send you a new one within the hour.
My licence expired. Can I still access my data?
Yes — your data is always on your computer and is never deleted. Renew your licence and enter the new key to continue using MUNIM.
I changed my PC. How do I move MUNIM?
Copy MUNIM.exe to the new PC. Copy the C:\MUNIM\ folder from the old PC to the new one. Log in with your existing username and password.
Something is not working. What should I do?
WhatsApp us with a screenshot of the error. We'll diagnose and fix it — usually on the same day.
