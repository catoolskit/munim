Quick answers

Common questions

How do I activate MUNIM after downloading?

Open MUNIM with internet ON and open or create your company. Enter the username, password and licence key we sent you. After that it works offline.

It says my licence is for a different product.

MUNIM Books and MUNIM Pro have separate licences. WhatsApp us and we'll check your licence right away.

I forgot my password.

WhatsApp us with your username and we'll help you get back in.

My licence expired. Is my data safe?

Yes — your data stays on your computer and is never deleted. After renewal, open MUNIM with internet ON and it continues automatically.

I changed my computer.

WhatsApp us to free your licence from the old computer. Copy the C:\MUNIM\ folder to the new one and activate with the same details.

How do I get the MUNIM mobile app?

WhatsApp us and we'll send you the mobile app and help you connect it to MUNIM Books.

Something is not working.

WhatsApp us a screenshot of the message. If MUNIM showed an error, the details are saved in C:\MUNIM\logs\ — send us the latest file from there.
