Sales Register

Create sales bills and watch them automatically post to the Sales Register, Party Ledger, and Trial Balance — in one click, just like Tally.

  • Create itemised sales bills with quantity, rate, and amount
  • Auto-calculates CGST, SGST, IGST based on GST rate
  • Bill auto-posts to Sales Register and Party Ledger
  • Print A4 GST invoice or thermal slip directly
  • View, edit, or cancel any past bill
  • Filter bills by date, party, or amount

Purchase Register

Record all purchases from suppliers. Every entry auto-posts to the Purchase Register, Supplier Ledger, and updates your stock automatically.

  • Record purchases with item, weight, purity, and rate
  • Supplier-wise purchase history at a glance
  • Auto-posts to Purchase Register and Supplier Ledger

  • Import purchase bills from PDF (coming soon)
  • Monthly and yearly purchase summary reports
