⚙️ Features

Everything in MUNIM

A complete list of what is built, what is coming, and how each feature works in practice.

Transactions
Recording your business
Every entry posts to the ledger automatically — no separate journal needed.
🧾
Sales Invoice
Create GST invoices for customers. CGST/SGST or IGST auto-detected from GSTIN state code.
  • Up to 5 line items per invoice
  • Multiple GST rates on one bill
  • Collect part payment at time of sale
  • Cash/Bank counter sales supported
  • Customer name on bill (walk-ins)
🛒
Purchase Bill
Record supplier purchases with Input GST auto-posted to ledger.
  • Input CGST/SGST/IGST auto-split
  • Pay supplier partially at time of entry
  • Links to outstanding for ageing
📔
Voucher Entry
Journal, Payment, Receipt and Contra vouchers. Full double-entry with any accounts.
  • Unlimited lines per voucher
  • Debit must equal credit — enforced
  • Type-to-search on account names
💰
Receipt / Payment
Record money received from customers or paid to suppliers. Auto-settles oldest bills first (FIFO).
  • Shows all open bills of the party
  • Excess stays as advance on account
  • Cash or Bank mode
📅
Daily Sales Summary
One entry for a full day's counter sales — amount per GST rate. For B2C retail only.
  • Tax-inclusive or tax-exclusive amounts
  • Cash or Bank receipt
  • Stored as a Sales voucher
Reports
Know your numbers instantly
All reports update in real time as you enter transactions.
📊
Trial Balance
Closing balances of every account. Prior years carried forward automatically. Export to Excel.
📈
Trading & P&L
Trading Account (gross profit) and P&L Account (net profit) in classic T-format. Opening and closing stock entered manually per year.
🏦
Balance Sheet
Assets and liabilities side by side. Profit carried automatically from P&L. Both sides always equal.
📒
Ledger View
Every entry in any account with a running balance. Opening brought forward from prior years.
💵
Cash Book
Cash and Bank accounts together with running balance. Same as Ledger View but for cash/bank only.
📋
Day Book
Every voucher for any date range, with all accounting lines. Double-click to open, edit or delete.
🧾
Sales & Purchase Register
All bills for the year with GST breakup, payment status (Paid / Partly paid / Unpaid). Export to Excel.
⏰
Outstanding & Ageing
Who owes you and whom you owe. Bill-wise or party-wise ageing with custom date ranges. Age from bill date or due date.
  • Custom ageing ranges (e.g. 30,60,90)
  • As-on date filter
  • Export to Excel
Masters
Set up your books
👥
Party Master
Customers and suppliers. Each gets their own ledger account automatically.
  • GSTIN with format validation
  • Opening balance
  • Credit days for due-date ageing
  • Add new party from invoice screen
📂
Ledger Accounts
Create accounts like Electricity, Freight, Commission. Choose group (Direct/Indirect Expense etc.) — determines where it appears in P&L.
  • 13 account groups supported
  • Opening balances
  • Delete unused accounts
Utilities & Safety
Keep your books safe
🔐
User Management
Admin and staff logins. Admin can create/delete staff users, reset passwords. Staff can enter transactions but not delete data.
📝
Audit Log
Every voucher created, edited or deleted is recorded — who did it, when, and what changed. Cannot be tampered with.
💾
Backup
One-click backup to any folder — USB drive, cloud folder, network drive. Gateway shows days since last backup in red if overdue.
🏢
Company Details
Business name, GSTIN, address, state, invoice prefix, round-off setting, books begin date. GST registered toggle hides tax fields if not applicable.
