How to use MUNIM Books

From first launch to monthly GST returns — follow these steps in order. Stuck anywhere? WhatsApp us and we'll walk you through it.

First day

Setting up MUNIM Books

Download and open

Keep MUNIM.exe in a permanent folder (for example D:\MUNIM App\) and double-click it. If Windows shows a blue warning, click More info → Run anyway. Accept the desktop shortcut — it updates itself if you move the file.

Activate (internet once)

Open or create your company. Enter the username, password and licence key we sent you on WhatsApp. After this, MUNIM works offline.

Set your own password

MUNIM asks you to choose a new password (8+ characters). Write it down somewhere safe.

Company details

Enter your business name, GSTIN, address and state. Not GST registered? Untick GST — tax fields hide automatically.

Utilities › Company Details

Customers and suppliers

Add each party with GSTIN, opening balance and credit days. You can also add a party later from the invoice screen with + New.

Masters › Party Master

Opening balances

Starting mid-year? Open any account and set its opening balance. For cash in hand, a Journal: Dr Cash, Cr Capital.

Masters › Ledger Accounts
Every day

Recording your business

A sale

Pick the customer, add items with quantity, rate and GST %. If paid now, fill Received now. Ctrl+S saves; Ctrl+P makes the PDF.

Transactions › Sales Invoice

Counter sales for the day

Many small cash sales without separate bills? Enter the day's total per GST rate in one go. Tick Amounts include GST if prices are tax-inclusive.

Transactions › Daily Sales Summary

A purchase

Pick the supplier and enter items with GST %. Input GST is posted automatically. If paid now, fill Paid now.

Transactions › Purchase Bill

Money received or paid later

Choose receipt or payment, pick the party and amount. The oldest open bill is settled first; any extra stays as advance.

Transactions › Receipt / Payment

An expense

Create the account once (e.g. Electricity under Indirect Expenses). Then a Payment voucher: Dr Electricity, Cr Cash.

Transactions › Voucher Entry

Fixing a mistake

Find the entry and open it to edit or delete. Every change is saved in the audit log.

Reports › Day Book
Every month

GST returns and checks

Check the Trial Balance

Debit equals credit means your entries are complete. Do this before filing.

Reports › Financial

Make GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B

Open each return for the month, review the figures and export the JSON file.

Reports › GST › G / 3

Upload and back up

Upload the JSON on the GST portal (or send it to your CA). Then take a backup to a USB drive.

Utilities › Backup

Good to know

Tips for clean books

B

Back up weekly

Save to a USB drive or cloud folder — not only on the same PC.

G

Invoice vs Daily Summary

Use a Sales Invoice for every GST-registered buyer. Daily Summary is for small B2C counter sales only. Confirm with your CA.

I

Stay licensed

Open MUNIM with internet at least once a month so it can re-check your licence quietly.
